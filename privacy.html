Privacy policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026.

mdm-shorts posts videos to the "Then vs Now Ranked" YouTube channel and TikTok account, and reads those accounts' statistics. Only the account owner signs in to it.

On YouTube it uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API. On TikTok it uses Login Kit and the Content Posting API, and reads the owner's basic profile, follower counts and video statistics.

The sign-in tokens and the statistics are stored only on the owner's computer. They are not sold, shared or sent to anyone else.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. For TikTok, see the TikTok Privacy Policy.

You can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions for Google, and under Settings, Security, Apps and services in the TikTok app.
